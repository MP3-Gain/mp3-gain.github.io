FAQ

Does it alter the audio quality?

No. Adjustments use metadata tags only—no re-encoding occurs.

What formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, WMA, WebM, FLAC, and most common audio types.

Is it safe for my originals?

Yes. Process copies or works non-destructively with backups recommended.

Why EBU R128?

It's the broadcast industry standard for consistent, ear-accurate loudness.
